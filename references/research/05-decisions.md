# Erik von Markovik (Mystery) - Major Decisions and Turning Points

## Overview

Erik von Markovik (born Erik James Horvat-Markovic, September 24, 1971) is a Canadian pickup artist, illusionist, and television personality. Known professionally as "Mystery," he created the Mystery Method and became one of the most influential figures in the seduction community.

---

## Timeline of Major Decisions

### 1. Early Life and Background (1971-1992)

**Context:**
- Born in Toronto, Ontario, Canada as Erik James Horvat-Markovic (with an accent over the C)
- Describes himself as a "late bloomer" with little success with women in early life
- Spent early years playing Dungeons & Dragons and honing magic skills
- First sexual experience at age 21 (late compared to peers)
- Fascinated by magic from youth; David Copperfield was an idol

**Decision Point: Name Change (Age 22, ~1993)**
- **Decision:** Legally changed name from Erik James Horvat-Markovic to Erik Von Markovik
- **Claimed Motive:** Stage name for illusionist shows
- **External Observation:** Creates a more theatrical, mysterious persona aligned with his magician identity
- **Source:** [Zulkey Interview](https://zulkey.com/) cited in Wikipedia
- **Credibility:** High - self-reported in interview

### 2. Magician to Pickup Artist Transition (Early-Mid 1990s)

**Background Context:**
- Worked as magician entertainer full-time with his own agent
- Performed magic at kids' parties and various venues
- His act was named "Natural Magic" (mentalist performances)
- Performed in Toronto, Las Vegas, and Hollywood

**Decision Point: Move to Los Angeles (~1999-2000)**
- **Decision:** Moved to LA to pursue television spot for his magic talent
- **Claimed Motive:** To become known as a magician in the US market
- **Outcome:** Money ran out before finding TV spot; had to return to Toronto
- **External Observation:** This failure may have contributed to pivot toward pickup instruction
- **Source:** [Sumptuous Capital Blog](https://blog.sumptuouscapital.com/mystery-dating-coach/)
- **Credibility:** Medium - secondary source

**Decision Point: Creating the Mystery Character (Late 1990s)**
- **Decision:** Created "Mystery" persona for mentalist performances titled "Natural Magic"
- **Claimed Motive:** Entertainment persona for magic shows
- **External Observation:** The character evolved into his PUA identity, blurring lines between entertainment and seduction instruction
- **Source:** Wikipedia, multiple sources
- **Credibility:** High

### 3. Entry into Seduction Community (1997)

**Decision Point: Joining alt.seduction.fast (April 1997)**
- **Decision:** First appeared in the pickup community forum alt.seduction.fast
- **Claimed Motive:** Share his theories after years of trial-and-error observation
- **Background:** Had spent 10+ years observing social interactions and testing approaches in clubs
- **External Observation:** His analytical approach distinguished him from other early PUAs like Ross Jeffries (who used NLP/hypnosis)
- **Source:** Multiple Chinese encyclopedia sources, community history
- **Credibility:** Medium-High - consistent across sources

**Decision Point: Developing Mystery Method (~1997-2004)**
- **Decision:** Created systematic approach to seduction (M3 Model: Attraction, Comfort, Seduction)
- **Claimed Logic:** Based on evolutionary psychology and field-tested interactions with thousands of women
- **Key Elements:**
  - Three-second rule (approach within 3 seconds)
  - Peacocking (eye-catching clothing/accessories)
  - Negging (backhanded compliments to lower target's perceived value)
  - Social proof theory
  - Cat string theory (pulling away makes target pursue)
- **Source:** [The Power Moves](https://thepowermoves.com/the-mystery-method/), The Game by Neil Strauss
- **Credibility:** High - well documented in his own book

### 4. Teaching and Mentorship Phase (2000-2004)

**Decision Point: First Live Instruction**
- **Decision:** Began offering live, in-field instruction - pioneered this format
- **Claimed Motive:** Share knowledge, help others like himself
- **Outcome:** Trained thousands of students worldwide; became a "Who's Who" of respected PUAs
- **External Observation:** This business model created revenue stream and expanded his influence
- **Source:** Mystery Method book introduction, [AskMystery.com](https://www.askmystery.com/)
- **Credibility:** High

**Decision Point: Teaching Neil Strauss (2003-2004)**
- **Decision:** Became mentor to Neil Strauss (journalist for Rolling Stone)
- **Claimed Motive:** Help a fellow seeker improve his social skills
- **Outcome:** Strauss documented this in The Game, becoming a NYT bestseller; Mystery became a household name
- **External Observation:** This relationship was pivotal - without Strauss's book, Mystery would have remained a niche figure
- **Source:** The Game by Neil Strauss, [Shortform Summary](https://www.shortform.com/blog/mystery-the-game/)
- **Credibility:** High - primary source (Strauss's book)

### 5. Business Formation (2004-2006)

**Decision Point: Founding Mystery Method Corporation (2004)**
- **Decision:** Formed partnership with Nick Savoy to create Mystery Method Corporation
- **Claimed Motive:** Professionalize and scale his teaching business
- **Outcome:** Company grew to a dozen trainers
- **Source:** [Dating Skills Review](https://www.datingskillsreview.com/mystery-method-corporation/)
- **Credibility:** High

**Decision Point: Stopping Regular Teaching (Mid-2005)**
- **Decision:** Stopped teaching in regular Mystery Method Corporation programs
- **Claimed Motive:** Not explicitly stated in sources
- **External Observation:** Possibly preparing for other ventures or conflicts with partner
- **Source:** Alchetron, Dating Skills Review
- **Credibility:** Medium

**Decision Point: Split from Mystery Method Corporation (December 2006)**
- **Decision:** Left Mystery Method Corporation with Lovedrop (Chris Odom) and James Matador (Stan Tayi)
- **Claimed Motive:** "Differences in opinion on how they should teach pickup artistry"
- **Outcome:** 
  - Founded Venusian Arts (August 2006)
  - Mystery Method Corporation continued as Love Systems (under Nick Savoy)
  - Legal battle over "Mystery Method" trademark and mysterymethod.com domain
- **External Observation:** Business dispute, not ideological - both sides continued similar teaching
- **Legal Context:** Nicholas Benedict filed suit against Mystery; trial calendared for October 20, 2008
- **Source:** [Dating Skills Review](https://www.datingskillsreview.com/mystery-erik-von-markovik/), [Archive.org PDF](https://archive.org/download/mysteryerikvonmarkovikchrisodommysterymethodrevelationvenusianartsebook2008/)

### 6. Project Hollywood Era (2004-2006)

**Decision Point: Joining Project Hollywood**
- **Decision:** Co-founded Project Hollywood mansion with Neil Strauss and others
- **Claimed Motive:** Create a seduction headquarters for PUAs, parties, seminars
- **Context:** Dean Martin's former Hollywood mansion
- **Outcome:**

  - Similar venues sprang up worldwide (Austin, San Francisco, Sydney, Perth)

- **External Observation:** The concept was flawed - expecting men dedicated to chasing women to coexist peacefully
- **Source:** [Shortform](https://www.shortform.com/blog/project-hollywood-the-game/), The Game
- **Credibility:** High - primary source

**Decision Point: Relationship with Katya**
- **Decision:** Got serious with Katya, who moved into Project Hollywood
- **Outcome:** Relationship fell apart; Katya got involved with another housemate
- **External Observation:** Love triangle caused constant drama; contributed to fracturing of Project Hollywood
- **Source:** Shortform summary
- **Credibility:** High - documented in The Game

### 7. Mental Health Crisis (~2005)

**Decision Point: Mental Breakdown**
- **Context:** Triggered by breakup with Patricia and codeine use
- **Behavior:** 
  - Erratic behavior and suicidal ideation
  - Stated: "This living thing is fubar" and "It's memetic. If you understand memetics, then you understand that it's all futile"
  - Planned to drown himself (what he most feared)
  - Described himself as "child robot, a sex robot, and an entertainer robot"
  - Gave away possessions, including computer to Patricia
  - Destroyed a phone, scattering its remnants
- **External Observation:** This crisis triggered community-wide "crisis of faith and self-examination" - many realized "the game" was negatively impacting their lives
- **Neil Strauss's Intervention:** Used psychiatrist's recommended sequence of questions to assess suicidal intent
- **Source:** [BookMaster AI](https://www.bookmaster.ai/neil-strauss/on/step-5-isolate-the-target/mysterys-mental-breakdown-and-community-crisis/)
- **Credibility:** High - documented in The Game

**Post-Crisis Reflection:**
- Scene in The Game shows Mystery crying in bathtub, questioning if anyone actually loves him or just loves the character he built
- **External Observation:** The man who claimed to have all answers for "getting the girl" struggled to find genuine connection himself
- **Source:** [Thomas Wictor Article](https://thomaswictor.com/erik-von-markovik-the-real-story-behind-mystery-the-pickup-artist-22ap/)
- **Credibility:** High - documented in The Game
